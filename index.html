How traversal works

The three traversal rows share one directional grammar: Earlier on the left, the axis control in the centre, and Later on the right. Narrow changes resolution, Step moves by exact seconds, and Previous or Next Mark moves through authored structure. Playback remains continuous. Widen returns in resolution, Undo returns in navigation history, and Exit returns to the parent Context.
