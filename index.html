Shift playback2× on Shift

Space plays Center at 1× with the Panorama cycling. Shift + Space plays Center alone at the Shift rate: the Panorama cannot hold its ratio at a changed rate, so Tail and Lead suspend for the duration. The fixed list offers exactly the rates this player reports it can play.

Following weight reads the map as a playback texture rather than correcting it: every doubling or halving of cumulative weight shifts the rate by one step. Weight 2× plays at 0.75×, weight 4× at 0.5×, weight 0.5× at 1.25×, and ground you never deformed plays at 1×. The Panorama keeps cycling alongside, one step either side of Center, and stands aside only at the ends of the rates this player offers. The rate changes as playback crosses Section boundaries.
